Big ideas start with a team.

BEST Robotics gives students a hands-on way to explore science, technology, engineering, and math. At Melissa High School, we bring different strengths to the same table, then turn sketches, code, and experiments into something that moves.

The people behind the power

Different talents. One mission.

There’s more than one way to build a robot. Here are a few of the roles that make a robotics team work. Add your student leaders and team photos here.

01 / ENGINEERING

Design & Build

Turn concepts into mechanisms, assemble components, and keep improving the physical robot.

02 / PROGRAMMING

Code & Controls

Make the robot respond reliably through logic, testing, and careful troubleshooting.

03 / CAD & STRATEGY

Plan the details

Model ideas, map out the game plan, and help the team make informed decisions.

04 / MARKETING

Tell our story

Create the website, pitch sponsors, design media, and show the community what we're learning.

05 / OUTREACH

Share the spark

Bring STEM to younger students and help more people see themselves as makers.

06 / EVERYONE

Make it happen

Organize, document, test, communicate, and help the whole team move forward together.
